Request account deletion

If you have a SKED account and no longer wish to use it, you can request deletion of your account and the personal data associated with it by contacting our support email below.

How to submit a request

  1. Send an email from the address associated with your SKED account, if possible.
  2. Use the subject line SKED Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address registered to your SKED account. Do not send your password.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.
Email SKED Support: REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-GMAIL@gmail.com

What happens after you request deletion?

After verifying your request, the SKED team will review it and delete the account and associated personal data that the service no longer needs, in accordance with applicable requirements. We will notify you when the request has been completed or if we need more information.

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained when required by law, needed to resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or protect the service. If information must be retained, it will be kept only for the applicable purpose and period.

Need help?

For questions about an account deletion request, contact the SKED support email shown above.

Note: Guest users who did not create a SKED account do not need to submit an account deletion request for an account. Any guest-session data is handled according to the app's applicable data-retention practices.
